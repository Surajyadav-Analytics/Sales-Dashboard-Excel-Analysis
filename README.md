# 📊 ABC Executive Summary & Profitability Dashboard

An interactive **Sales Analytics Dashboard built in Microsoft Excel** to analyze revenue, profitability, product performance, and regional sales. This dashboard provides a consolidated executive summary to support data-driven business decisions.

## 📸 Dashboard Preview

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze overall sales performance and profitability.
- Track monthly revenue and profit trends.
- Identify top-performing countries and products.
- Compare sales performance across different regions.
- Understand product category contributions to total sales.
- Provide an interactive view of business performance.

## 📊 Dashboard Features

- **Monthly Sales Analysis:** Track revenue and profit trends throughout the year.
- **Country Performance:** Compare the top five countries by revenue and profit.
- **Product Category Analysis:** Visualize sales distribution across product categories.
- **Regional Sales Analysis:** Compare sales contributions across different regions.
- **Top 5 Products:** Identify products generating the highest sales.
- **Geographical Analysis:** Explore revenue distribution across countries using a map.
- **Interactive Filters:** Analyze results by region, sales channel, and quarter.
- **Executive KPI Cards:** Monitor important business metrics at a glance.
